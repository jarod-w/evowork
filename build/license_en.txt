1. Local-First Execution & Data Ownership
- File parsing, command execution, office document generation, and scheduled tasks run locally on your computer. Document parsing never uploads your raw files to the cloud.
- Tasks, workspace artifacts, automations, and local indexes belong to your OS user account on this machine. Signing out or deleting a cloud account does not remove your local data.

2. Network Boundaries & Explicit Consent
- Without signing in, you may use EvoWork with your own model API keys (BYOK); the client makes zero background requests to EvoWork servers while signed out unless explicitly enabled.
- Model inference requires network access and sends your prompt and selected context to the model provider you choose. Requests routed through the EvoWork gateway are used only for protocol adaptation and aggregate usage metering; prompts and responses are never persisted to disk.
- Sharing artifacts, enabling external connectors, and downloading updates happen only upon your explicit action or configured preference.
